# Unit Economics for TrackSpend AI

## What a Converted Lead Is Worth to Credex

The ideal customer is a startup or engineering team already spending heavily on:
- AI subscriptions,
- SaaS tooling,
- cloud infrastructure,
- and developer workflows.

These users are likely candidates for:
- AI infrastructure financing,
- startup credit products,
- vendor financing,
- or procurement optimization services.

### Assumption

If Credex helps a startup optimize and finance:
- AI tooling,
- cloud credits,
- or software procurement,

a realistic annual revenue per converted customer could be:

| Revenue Source | Estimated Annual Revenue |
|---|---|
| Financing margin / fees | $1,200 |
| SaaS procurement commissions | $500 |
| Upsell services | $300 |

### Estimated Annual Value per Customer

```text
≈ $2,000/year per converted account
```


# What Would Need to Be True

## 1. AI Spend Pain Keeps Growing

Startups continue adopting:
- ChatGPT,
- Claude,
- Cursor,
- Windsurf,
- Perplexity,
- AI APIs.

AI tooling costs must become operationally painful.

---

## 2. Audit Results Must Feel Accurate

Users must trust:
- savings estimates,
- overlap detection,
- and recommendations.

If outputs feel generic, conversion drops immediately.

---

## 3. Distribution Must Stay Founder-Led

The product works best when distributed through:
- founder communities,
- engineering Twitter/X,
- AI Discords,
- and startup Slack groups.

Not traditional enterprise sales.

---

## 4. Credex Must Monetize Beyond the Audit

The audit itself is a lead-generation engine.

Revenue likely comes from:
- financing,
- procurement optimization,
- vendor partnerships,
- or infrastructure credit products.

---

# Key Insight

The biggest advantage is that the acquisition channel already contains:
- highly technical,
- high-spending,
- AI-native buyers.

Most users discovering the tool already:
- pay for multiple AI subscriptions,
- experiment aggressively,
- and publicly discuss tooling problems.

That makes the lead quality unusually strong compared to traditional SaaS funnels.
