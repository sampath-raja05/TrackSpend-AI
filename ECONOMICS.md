# Economics

Assumption: a converted TrackSpend AI lead buys discounted AI credits or infrastructure access with an annual gross margin contribution of roughly $2,000-$8,000 depending on contract size. A small startup spending $2,000/month on AI tools might shift $12,000-$24,000/year through TrackSpend AI; at a rough 15%-25% gross margin, that is $1,800-$6,000 in gross profit. I would use $3,000 as a base converted-lead value until real CRM data says otherwise.

Channel CAC can start near zero in cash but not zero in labor. Founder/community outreach might take 20 personalized messages for one completed audit and 100 for one qualified call. If founder time is valued at $75/hour and 100 messages take 5 hours, CAC to consultation is around $375. A strong Show HN or community launch could produce 100 audits from 6 hours of work, maybe 5 calls, so consultation CAC is about $90 in labor.

A workable funnel: 100 visitors -> 35 audits completed -> 12 emails captured -> 3 consultations booked -> 1 credit purchase. If each purchase is worth $3,000 gross profit, the product can support meaningful founder-led acquisition. Paid acquisition would need stricter math: at $4 CPC, 100 visitors cost $400; using the same funnel, one purchase at $3,000 gross profit is profitable, but only if traffic quality is high.

To drive $1M ARR in 18 months, the tool needs either 333 customers worth $3,000 ARR each or 100 larger customers worth $10,000 ARR each. That requires repeatable acquisition beyond one-off launches: SEO pages for every major AI tool pricing comparison, partner distribution with AI finance/accounting advisors, and a sales-assisted workflow for audits over $500/month in identified savings.
